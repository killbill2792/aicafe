## Source → object key → storage → Supervisor

1. Existing `purchase_orders` and `purchase_order_lines` are the canonical **owner-approved internal PO**. Use `(business_id,id)` and the immutable line snapshots; `status=approved_for_ordering` does **not** mean emailed or supplier accepted.
2. Existing `cafe_suppliers` is the supplier identity, scoped by `business_id,id`. Its name is not an email address. A new owner-entered/verified `cafe_supplier_order_contacts` row stores `(business_id,supplier_id,id)`, destination email, owner verification, creation and revocation. Search results, Gmail history and AI guesses may suggest a contact for the owner to verify, but never create a trusted contact automatically.
3. Existing owner-scoped Gmail connector reports account ID, account generation, email, send/read permissions and status. Existing Gmail search/read may find a *verified historical conversation*; absence of Gmail history is missing evidence, **not** evidence that the supplier is unknown, uncooperative or an order has not been placed by phone, portal or another channel.
4. New `purchase_order_dispatches`, uniquely keyed by `(business_id,purchase_order_id)`, records the exact immutable PO snapshot hash, recipient contact, authorized proposal/effect hash, source Gmail account/generation, send reservation, outcome, provider receipt and supplier acknowledgment. A separate immutable event/exception trail records provider uncertainty and owner replies. Never replace an uncertain send with another send.
5. Supervisor reads PO+dispatch state through the **existing purchasing reader** and registered tools. From approved PO, it investigates the supplier's channel using permitted data, prepares exact order email, requests a **separate owner-click send approval** and uses the existing Supervisor effect gateway + Gmail provider. Gmail's verified SENT receipt proves Gmail accepted the message, **not** that the supplier received, confirmed, priced or fulfilled the order.
6. Existing durable worker / routines + owner chat/inbox report unresolved communication. If no Gmail account, no historical conversation, no verified supplier email, no send permission, changed authorization, provider timeout or no supplier response: proactively tell the owner the exact known/unknown facts, ask which channel/contact they use and what outcome was reached, preserve answers as explicitly attributed owner-reported evidence, and offer the next permitted action. Never silently mark complete.
7. No portal, phone or EDI send adapter in 14C; those paths are `owner_action_required` until a verified authorized connector exists. No autonomous negotiation, order modifications, monetary transactions or inventory receipt.
