## Intent and alternatives
Owner requests a substantial redesign, not card polish. ChatGPT-like simplicity, Claude-like typography, Grok-like responsiveness, Muse-like durable work. No competitor integration or new agent. Reject a cosmetic refresh (nested shell and competing surfaces remain), and reject a new standalone application (duplicates state, auth and capability paths). Recommend an adaptive conversation-first shell over the same routes, APIs and durable records. Mobile is the primary design; desktop adds an optional rail, not a second application.

| Incomplete or stale source / other café | Seed explicit missing/null/stale coverage and separately authenticated foreign owner | Honestly unavailable or partial; no misleading chart, no foreign records, no fake completion |
