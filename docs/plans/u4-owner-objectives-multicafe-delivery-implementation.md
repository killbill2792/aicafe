# Luna operational intelligence — one-time objectives, multi-café recovery and unified owner delivery

**Owner-approved implementation scope, 2026-11-21:** One independent draft PR; build on merged U0–U5, no other agent, no Temporal/Inngest/OpenClaw/Grok/Muse runtime, no duplicated queues, separate CRM, independent scheduler or question-specific business scripts. Owner authorizes development implementation and deterministic testing, **not** production flags, database migrations applied to live production, paid Luna, direct staff/supplier effects or release. **Task17 is explicitly out of scope.** Keep U4 Watch disabled in production; it may be tested with disposable fixtures.

## Outcome the owner must actually experience

1. Owner: "At 4:03 PM café time, look at sales so far, compare with yesterday, investigate anything notable, and tell me." Luna saves **the complete authenticated original goal**, café/timezone, one due occurrence and short chat confirmation. No automatic interpretation as "send back the reminder title".
2. Without an open chat, the due-work dispatcher claims that saved occurrence, real Luna selects authorized current café-data tools, independently verifies evidence, records a complete result once, and creates **one** owner Work/Operations Inbox report. Chat gets **one short line** ("Sales review finished" with a link), not the full report or fake progress. A permitted opt-in installed PWA gets a short push linked to that same report.
3. At scale, a café with a slow, failed, revoked or stale source does **not** delay unrelated cafés; work is safely retried/reclaimed, source-read errors are observed, and no duplicate report, provider call or external effect is asserted. A simple personal reminder remains a minimal reminder and is not forced into expensive research.
4. Completed/superseded/expired information disappears from **active** attention surfaces appropriately while auditable results and genuine still-open work remain accessible separately. An unknown task payload or missing locale string cannot crash Operations/Café Profile.

## The scalable multi-café execution design

1. **One cheap scheduler admission:** Reuse \`/api/cron/refresh-derived\` as the single existing schedule entrypoint for this capability; make its interval **minute-level** so arbitrary due times do not wait 15 minutes. Time-critical admission must be cheap and completed before any heavy work. Perform original derived-data refresh on its **existing approximately 15-minute cadence** (time/lease-based gated, not once per minute). No WhatsApp cron reuse. No separate queue/agent/service/platform.
2. **Claim due work, do not scan/execute every café serially:** SQL selects small indexed batches ordered by due time and fair last-served café cursor; atomically claims with expiring leases, backoff, idempotency and limited concurrent work globally **and per café**. The saved café scope, source revision and owner grant are rechecked by the worker. Never trust the model to choose tenant IDs. A busy café's claim cap must not exhaust global throughput.
3. **Bound every step:** existing refresh, incident and owner-objective code each has bounded per-call/tool budget; admission returns or yields before Vercel's hard 300s. Long source read/provider failure is classified and deferred; peer cafés continue in *independent bounded work units*, not one \`await\` chain. Use \`Promise.allSettled\` for genuinely independent leased slots only with real propagated timeouts/cancellation; a pretend JS timeout around an unstoppable call does not free resources.
4. **Restart/reclaim:** A dead function/worker's lease expires; next invocation reclaims with the *same* \`run_id\`, original objective/source identity, checkpoint, revision and spent budget. Verify sources afresh; no re-use of stale unsupported numbers. Transactional final report + Inbox/chat pointer and once-only dedupe. Already delivered push may need at-least-once transport semantics; record transport attempt and dedupe, never promise exactly-once device display.
5. **Fairness/overload:** global and per-café concurrent budgets, oldest-due scheduling with fair rotating café claim, retry-after-aware exponential backoff + jitter for transient errors, bounded attempts and actionable failure visibility. Never silently abandon a due objective or have one repeatedly failing café monopolize every tick.
6. **Operational observability:** logs/metrics for due lag p50/p95, queue depth by state, oldest eligible work, per-café fairness (aggregated/anonymized), attempts, claim age/reclaim, time spent in source/AI/delivery, retry reasons, 300s timeouts, owner action latency. No private prompts, owner names or POS payment metadata in logs. Use old production backlog for **diagnosis**, not as a required bulk-cleanup/replay workload.

## Intelligent boundaries — avoid a business rules engine

- Luna's **model**, not keyword triggers or category-to-template maps, determines whether an owner asks for a simple human nudge or actual source-backed investigation, which registered tool/read to call, which missing evidence to research, the follow-up question, recommendation and when a business condition is no longer actionable.
- Remove business-family canned owner questions in \`lib/ai/supervisor/routines/investigation.server.ts\` and \`lib/ai/supervisor/routines/investigation.ts\` only when they override model reasoning. Prefer model's actual verified unanswered question and persisted source gap; if the model cannot verify, say precisely missing/unknown and keep work pending rather than invent an evidence-based question or marking it complete.
- Preserve deterministic **non-business** enforcement: registered read tool permissions; source-as-of, authentic receipts and canonical metrics; tenant isolation; independent numeric verification; token/model budgets; approval-only external effects; injected/hostile tool data untrusted; no direct staff or supplier outreach.
- Proactive U4 Watch remains gated OFF in production until explicit release approval; test Watch in disposable/non-production only. No additional watch engine and no mandatory fresh model call for a source state already proved unchanged.
