## 1. Problem and evidence

AI CAFE must not create a second published menu alongside an authenticated Toast-connected café. Onboarding should start with the Toast connection and automatically populate real published products, sizes, modifiers, prices, and category/group identity; the owner then contributes recipes, ingredient quantities/costs, and other enrichment. Today's code mixes manual and Toast items in the Menu's All/Needs attention/Archived view, supports local Add Item as immediately active, and can locally edit POS-controlled fields without writing them back to Toast.

Read-only audit, connected verified café on Nov 7: 57 Toast canonical rows (53 active priced across 45 base groups, four inactive), and one extra locally active manual/testing product. Only two active Toast rows have recipe lines and 19 cost fallback values; a fallback is not verified ingredient cost. These observations do NOT establish live published source freshness. Old testing rows and their recipes can remain in storage solely to preserve historical dependencies, not as another current Menu, a mandatory owner-review queue, or an obligation to transfer their recipes.

Historical Aug 22–Sep 28 sales: 563 Toast order lines across seven base GUIDs have null canonical menu_item_id, **not missing original sales**. All 563 have a stable POS line ID and matching retained raw order selection; 506 include original 12 oz/16 oz modifier labels and a historical group GUID not matching the current published size group. No automatic current-recipe assignment is justified by title, price, or modifier label alone. The existing menu importer may issue a duplicate full Menus V2 GET and may deactivate a provider-owned row based on an inadequately verified snapshot; those are POS0d correctness risks, not permission to create a parallel importer.

## 2. Goals, non-goals and anti-goals

**Goals (owner approved):**
1. Verified Toast publication is authoritative for what appears in the connected café's current published menu: official provider product/size/option/group identity, names, category/group, selling prices, availability and active publication state.
2. A new café can connect Toast with **zero owner-created menu rows**; its published products and their real options become visible without manual recreation.
3. Display **one provider-identity product group with its authentic published sizes/options** beneath it. Each size retains its own GUID, price and independently enriched recipe. Show a size label once, not twice.
4. Allow owner-managed recipes, ingredients, quantities, costs and approved presentation/enrichment without claiming those were published into Toast.
5. For a verified Toast café, **no AI CAFE local Add Item draft or Owner review menu**. Create/edit/size/price/availability and publish the product in Toast Web; AI CAFE reads the published change through the existing version-checked Toast sync. Connected-café Add Item should be replaced/hidden with clear “Manage/Add in Toast, then publish” guidance. AI CAFE does not write menu updates into Toast.
6. Preserve old manual/testing and CSV rows/recipes, historical order/price/ingredient dependencies, and archived Toast variants in database for D3 safety only; do not surface them in the connected-café current Menu, do not force recipe migration and do not delete/merge until separately authorized dependency/rollback proof. Disconnected/CSV cafés retain their supported manual flow; confirmed missing Toast item becomes inactive (not hard-deleted), and same GUID republished reactivates.
7. Use existing change-driven menu metadata and an already scheduled coordinator, **not full menu polling every 10 minutes**, a second catalog, or a second scheduler. Failed/partial evidence never removes good menu items.
8. Menu screens, customer/published catalog and the **one Supervisor** apply the same source-authority/current-published distinction, while historical answers preserve source/as-of context.

**Non-goals:** Toast writeback; a connected-café local Add Item or unpublished menu draft; Owner review tab for old manual test products; a second catalog; automatic legacy recipe transfer; deleting historic dependencies; title/price-based old size guesses; D4B implementation from this spec.

**Anti-goals:** Local testing/manual product appearing in current Toast Menu, AI CAFE Add Item pretending to publish in Toast, stale/partial response unpublishing good products, recipe attached to wrong size, selling price mistaken for known ingredient cost, or loss of disconnected café manual behavior.

## 3. Owner-approved architecture

~~~mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Verified Toast connection] --> B[Existing 10-minute coordinator checks lightweight version/metadata]
  B -->|unchanged| C[Keep last verified published catalog]
  B -->|changed or bounded repair| D[One complete restaurant-scoped Menus V2 candidate]
  D --> E{Complete, consistent and authorized?}
  E -->|no| C
  E -->|yes| F[Existing catalog sync by Toast GUID; price/version evidence]
  F --> G[Published Toast-only Menu projection + current-menu Supervisor]
  H[Owner adds or changes product in Toast Web] --> J[Owner publishes in Toast]
  J --> B
  E1[Owner optionally edits recipe or ingredient cost on existing Toast product] --> K{Exact verified café + Toast size GUID?}
  K -->|yes| L[Enrich that Toast product; keep history]
  K -->|no| N[No local product creation or guessed recipe]
  L --> G
  F --> M[Confirmed missing Toast item: inactive; historical records preserved]
~~~

**Current published identity:** scope by verified business + Toast restaurant + actual Toast base item GUID; size identity is the exact Toast option GUID within its source group. Reuse existing menu_items, provider IDs/aliases, modifier_recipes, menu_item_modifier_groups, menu_price_history, toast_catalog_facts/versions and existing raw/current order evidence where appropriate. **Do not create a second raw menu/archive or competing published table.** The one current published Toast projection, existing readers and any metadata require a dependency audit and explicit later design acceptance; no Owner review projection/menu or local test-product migration is authorized.

**Source-boundary rule — CURRENT Toast product identity is automatic, historic manual testing recipe migration is NOT required:** Toast controls source products, official names/categories, true size GUIDs/prices and active state. Group and import by exact café+restaurant+Toast GUID; offer optional recipes/ingredients/costs only on already verified Toast products. Do not invent a local menu draft or a separate Owner review queue and do not move old testing recipes based on name/price. Unambiguous existing Toast-linked enrichment can remain; any future transfer needs reversible explicit source evidence. This latest decision supersedes the former automatic migration proposal for old manually entered test products.

**Current Menu/screen behavior, binding Nov 8 correction:** Verified Toast café All/search/category/recipe-cost attention/customer menu and current-menu Supervisor show ONLY the validated published Toast source. Archived retains original real Toast variants for history. Existing manually active test rows are absent from these current surfaces and no dedicated Owner review tab is required. Connected Add Item is hidden/replaced by “Manage/Add in Toast, then publish”; direct local create/rename/price/status actions cannot spoof publication. Owner recipe/cost enrichment is editable only on verified Toast item+size, unknown costs shown honestly missing. Disconnected/CSV café manual flow continues. **SUPERSEDED:** the earlier proposed unpublished local Add Item draft and Owner review tab are expressly rejected in [#6727941284](https://github.com/killbill2792/aicafe/pull/398#issuecomment-6727941284).

**Publication/removal:** A complete authenticated, restaurant-scoped, consistent published snapshot can add/update active Toast options. A disappearance may mark provider publication inactive **only after completeness and independent confirmation**; preserve all historical identities, prices, recipes and order lines. Republish with the same GUID reactivates. Empty/partial/stale/unpermitted/mismatched-version/missing referenced groups, publication lag or failed write is **not proof of removal**. Keep last-good published state and visible stale/failure evidence. No auto-delete; any owner-requested hard removal requires separate explicit approval and historical-dependency validation, with historical evidence retained.

**Historical sales:** Original Toast order item/selection/modifier GUIDs are authoritative for what was purchased on that date. Today's 12 oz/24 oz catalog is **not** proof of an older 16 oz recipe. Keep all 563 source sales queryable; reconcile to historical variants only with dated source/alias/owner-confirmed evidence, not title or amount guesses. Incomplete size→recipe relationships make dependent ingredient usage, cost/COGS and profit **partial**, never fabricated zero/fully verified.

## 5. Failure handling and STRIDE threat model

| STRIDE / failure | Risk | Required boundary / test |
|---|---|---|
| Spoofing | Owner manually claims Toast-published identity | Publication only from verified restaurant-scoped Toast API + durable validated snapshot; no draft-to-published toggle |
| Tampering | Local action overwrites Toast name/price/availability or steals GUID | Server action and DB-level source rules; tenant-scoped identity/alias validation; no silent recipe transfer |
| Repudiation | Owner or sync cannot explain draft match/unpublish/republish | Auditable confirmation, source restaurant/version, action actor, as-of evidence, retained previous state |
| Information disclosure | Another café's menu/recipes show in review or Supervisor | Authenticated active-business scope, per-location authorization, RLS and negative cross-café tests |
| Denial of service / data loss | Partial/menu-empty/stale response retires real item; two GETs create churn | Version/completeness guard, independent disappearance verification, last-good, bounded retry, rate limit adherence |
| Elevation of privilege | Direct link/server action publishes draft or edits provider-owned fields | Source-aware backend gates, owner roles, RLS/privileged RPC review, no security-by-hidden-tab |
| Integrity / historical mismatch | Old size modifiers linked by display name/price | Historical GUID/version proof; explicit unknown cost status; no rewriting original transaction money |

## 8.1 Cross-domain boundary: proposed price and staff experiments (owner clarified 2026-11-07)

The owner does not operate Toast by editing the AI CAFE menu. **AI CAFE is not a Toast write-back interface.** This is binding to D4C menu-price authority and a dependency for a **separate in-review experiment-activation design** [specs/0002-pos-change-verified-experiment-activation.md](0002-pos-change-verified-experiment-activation.md). For a connected Toast café:
- Keep **published Toast price** distinct from **proposed/target price** and **owner-entered local notes**. Do not immediately write an experiment's target to `menu_items.price_cents` or assert an official change happened. Only a verified published Toast menu change can advance the current Toast price, under the normal version-gated sync.
- When an owner approves an experiment, Supervisor remembers its target, duration and approval, provides clear instructions to change and **publish** in Toast, and remains **awaiting Toast verification**. A pending difference from a proposal is an expected, actionable *pending implementation*, not a data corruption alert, not zero/missing sales and not a valid reason to discard records.
- Observe the actual Toast publication for the exact café and item/size GUID and target price. Only after durable source-backed price verification can a Toast price experiment become **active** and its requested measured-day window start. If the owner clicks **Done** but Toast still reports the prior price, remind the owner: “We agreed to test this price, but Toast still shows the previous published price. Please update and publish it in Toast; the test has not started.” Track reminders without spamming; never fabricate dates.
- Staff scheduling/roster experiments follow the **same source-applied-versus-AI-CAFE-proposed principle**, using their real authoritative system and verified effective evidence; do not claim a schedule change occurred just because a local plan was saved. Exact staff capability and acknowledgment policies are separate design questions.
- Preserve actual sales amounts and dated source price; experimentation metadata never overwrites historical POS truth. Screen, price recommendation, Supervisor and experiment reporting distinguish `proposed`, `waiting_for_toast`, `verified_active`, `complete` and `stale/unavailable` where supported.

**Confirmed code defect to assign, not yet fixed:** `lib/ai/supervisor/loop/priceExperiment.server.ts` correctly commits `requires_manual_action`, but `app/api/ai/threads/[threadId]/experiments/route.ts` action `manual_done` currently calls `confirmManualLoop` with the next local calendar date **without reading a published Toast price**. Separately, `lib/ai/supervisor/loop/menuPriceChange.server.ts` commits via `setCanonicalMenuPrice` and reports `verified` on the **local** price history, even if the source Toast price did not change. Owner's target behavior requires an actual provider-confirmation gate. These defects are NOT solved by D4C display filtering alone and must receive their own owned implementation scope, migrated DB and registered Supervisor tests. If the source is unavailable or a menu has unpublished changes, keep waiting/unverified. Official Toast guidance: [Menus API returns published data only](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/devguide/apiMenusApiReturnsPublishedDataOnly.html) and [check /metadata before fetching](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/devguide/apiEnsuringYourMenuDataIsUpToDate.html).
